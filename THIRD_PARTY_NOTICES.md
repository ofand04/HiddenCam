# Third-party project references

The implementation was informed by a technical review of the following independent open-source Android projects:

- FadCam: https://github.com/anonfaded/FadCam — GPL-3.0.
- Valv-Android: https://github.com/Arctosoft/Valv-Android — GPL-3.0.

No source files or compiled artifacts from those repositories are bundled in HiddenCam. Their licensing terms apply to their respective repositories. The HiddenCam source files remain distributed under the license included in `LICENSE`.
